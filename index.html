Florida Secondary Metals Statutes (F.S. § 538.18 – § 538.26) DUVAL COUNTY ENFORCEMENT

• Valid Government ID & Thumbprint (F.S. § 538.19): All Jacksonville scrap yards are required by state law to capture a valid driver's license/ID, vehicle tag number, digital photograph, and right thumbprint for regulated non-ferrous transactions.

• Restricted Metals & Check Hold Rules (F.S. § 538.235 / § 538.26): Cash payouts are prohibited for air conditioner evaporator coils, catalytic converters, utility wire, and manhole covers unless accompanied by an active HVAC/contractor license or commercial proof of ownership. Regulated items are paid via mailed check or verified business account.

• Catalytic Converter Anti-Theft Act: Possession or sale of detached catalytic converters without documented VIN/title proof or registered repair shop credentials is a felony under Florida law.
