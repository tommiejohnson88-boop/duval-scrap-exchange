Florida Statute § 538 - Duval County Scrap Law Requirements

All Duval County scrapyards require a valid government-issued photo ID, vehicle license plate log, and digital thumbprint before payout. Restricted secondary metals (AC copper condensing coils, burnt insulated copper wire, catalytic converters, manhole covers, bronze cemetery markers) require proof of commercial HVAC/contractor licensing or payout will be held via check.
